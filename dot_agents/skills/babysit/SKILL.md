name: babysit
description: Babysit the PRs and branches relevant to the current thread.
---

look at the prs and branches relavent to this thread and babysit them, this means going through them and making sure:

- They're in a mergable state
- They have no merge conflicts
- They have no unresolved comments
- This includes comments that maybe in the description
- You should reply to every comment, even if there's no fix
- Only implement fixes if the problem is serious or trivial to fix
